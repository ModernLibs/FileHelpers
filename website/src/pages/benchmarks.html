Summary

  • The fastest fixed-width reader in our benchmarks: more than twice as fast as FileHelpers 3.5.2 and ahead of CsvHelper 34.
  • On par with the best CSV readers: reading a 25-column file, ahead of Sep and CsvHelper 34 at every size we measured, level with Sylvan at 1M rows and behind it at 10K and 100K.
  • Behind on two things: Sep, Sylvan and CsvHelper read quoted fields faster, and CsvHelper writes faster.

Why it is fast

The read path works on ReadOnlySpan<char>: lines and fields are sliced, not copied, and numeric, date and other value-type fields are parsed straight from the span. ReadFile parses records as it reads the file instead of loading it into one string, buffers are pooled, and the quote scan uses vector search. Struct records with value-type fields are read without per-record allocations, and the test suite checks that.

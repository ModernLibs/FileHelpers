# ModernLibs.FileHelpers 4.10.0 benchmarks (draft)

## Safe-code audit

Source: Step 0 round step0-2026-11-12, per-VM report sections. Ratio is `Scan_SpanCreate` / `Scan_LoadUnsafe` (safe span
loads over unsafe `LoadUnsafe` loads); above 1.0 means the safe form is slower. Intervals are 99.9 %.

| Runtime | Dataset | VM | Ratio [interval] | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| .NET 10 | LongLines | `fh-amd` | 1.098 [1.085, 1.307] | slower |
| .NET 10 | LongLines | `fh-intel` | 1.156 [1.101, 1.161] | slower |
| .NET 11 | LongLines | `fh-intel` | 1.121 [1.096, 1.168] | slower |
| .NET 10 | Quoted | `fh-intel` | 1.055 [1.033, 1.059] | slower |

All other cases of the round read no change.

Decision: the spec condition "no loss on .NET 10 and on .NET 11, on x64 and Arm64" fails on x64, so the scanner loads
stay and Arm64 numbers are not needed.

`AllowUnsafeBlocks` is removed from `ModernLibs.FileHelpers.csproj`: the library has no `unsafe` block or pointer, and
the full check passes without it. `Unsafe.As`, `MemoryMarshal.GetReference` and `LoadUnsafe` calls remain in
`QuoteBalanceScanner` (`Parsing/QuoteBalanceScanner.cs` lines 140, 148, 165 at this commit). Other `Unsafe` / `MemoryMarshal` uses at this
commit: `Parsing/RecordTokenizer.cs` lines 37 and 146 (`MemoryMarshal.Cast`), `Engines/FileHelperEngine.cs` lines 1182
and 1208 (`Unsafe.SizeOf`), `Descriptors/RecordParsing.cs` lines 146 to 148 (`Unsafe.ByteOffset`,
`MemoryMarshal.GetReference`). None needs `AllowUnsafeBlocks`.

Step 2 (the F5 change to `ScanSingleQuote`) waits for the F5 verdict from the T19 round; nothing changes here until then.
